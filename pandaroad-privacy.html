Privacy Policy

Panda Road — effective 24 August 2026

The short version: Panda Road collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data ever leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. Panda Road has no sign-in, no servers of its own, and makes no network requests. We do not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or location.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so it is there when you come back: your best score, furthest journey, lifetime totals, the current contract, and the result of today's Daily Journey. This never leaves your phone and is removed if you delete the app.

Third parties

Panda Road contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Children

Panda Road is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
