How to play

Drag items from the tray into either basket. The panda's pole tips according to weight and distance from his shoulders, so where you set something down matters as much as what it is. Keep the pole level.

The chime

Hold the pole within a couple of degrees of level for a moment and a chime rings. Consecutive chimes build a chain that multiplies everything you carry, so precise placement is worth far more than simply loading up.

Villages

The panda stops at stalls along the road. The requested items glow in your baskets — tap them to hand them over. Deliveries pay well, lighten the load, and sometimes earn a gift you can carry: a rope coil that lashes cargo down, a feather that lightens a basket, or a lantern that widens the chime window.

The road

Slopes tilt the pole, so lean your load uphill. Gusty stretches sway it. Cobblestones shake cargo loose. Low bridges scrape the top off a tower built too tall — tilting can duck one side under. Rain melts ice faster, snow slows the walk.

Daily Journey

One seeded road per day, the same for everyone, one attempt. Your score for the day is kept in the Ledger.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. Panda Road is fully offline.

Where is my progress kept? On your device only. Deleting the app erases it.

Are there ads or purchases? No — none of either.

Can I turn the sound off? Yes, with the speaker button in the top right.
