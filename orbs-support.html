ORBS — Support

Two games, one app: BLAST levels and endless LINK.

Contact

For help, feedback, or bug reports, email durukan_al@yahoo.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

How to play

BLAST — tap a group of two or more same-colour orbs to pop it. Each level has a goal: clear the board, collect a colour, or break all the stones. Big pops (6+) leave a power-up behind — a bomb clears a 3×3, a rocket clears its whole row or column, and power-ups chain into each other. Frozen orbs melt when you pop a group next to them. Finish with moves to spare for more stars.

LINK — endless mode. Drag a line through three or more touching same-colour orbs; longer chains score more.

Coins & boosters

Winning levels and daily gifts earn coins. Spend them in a level on the Hammer (smash any one piece), Shuffle (re-mix the board), or +5 Moves when you run out.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress — there are no accounts or cloud saves in this version.

Privacy

ORBS collects no data. See the privacy policy.
