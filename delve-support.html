Frequently asked

Does he really keep going when the app is closed? Yes. He mines, descends and levels up while the app is shut, capped at 8 hours by default. The Hibernation skill, the Deep Echo relic and the Ascetic class each raise that cap. When you come back, the game tells you where he got to.

What is the dive, and why did mine reset? A dive runs from the moment he goes down until you bring him up, and the longer it runs the better he finds — a full one doubles your loot luck. Looking costs you nothing: the widget, opening the app, reading the log, fighting a Warden all leave it running. It ends only when you work on his kit — equipping, selling, salvaging, enhancing or reforging — or when you Ascend.

I'm stuck and can't go deeper. That is the game asking you to Ascend. It restarts the dungeon, not your character: you keep your level, attributes, skills, class, lumen and dust, and gain Relics to spend on the constellation. Almost everyone who feels permanently stuck has not Ascended yet.

A Warden is too strong. Every Warden has a law printed under its name, and the law tells you what it wants — matched armour, an enhanced edge, spread power, spent gold. Answer the law rather than only raising Power.

My progress disappeared. Saves live on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud. Deleting the app or clearing storage can lose a local save. If a device save looks wrong, the game offers to restore an iCloud one when it finds a deeper run. Email us if something still looks off.

Is any personal data collected? No. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. See the Privacy Policy.
