PACK IT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Short version: PACK IT does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.

What we collect

Nothing. PACK IT is a single-player puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. It does not require an account, a login, or any personal information to play.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — completed levels, stars earned, settings, and unlocked items — is saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and is removed if you delete the app.

Analytics and advertising

This version of PACK IT contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. The app makes no network requests to collect information about you and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

Data sharing

Because no personal data is collected, none is sold or shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

PACK IT is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update introduces any data collection (for example, optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
