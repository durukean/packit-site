Frequently asked

How do I earn 3 stars? Fill as much of the box as you can and make sure nothing breaks on the drive. 1 star = it fits, 2 stars = nothing broke, 3 stars = a near-perfect pack.

My progress disappeared. Progress is saved on your device. It can be lost if the app is deleted or device storage is cleared. Email us if something looks wrong.

Is there any personal data collected? No — PACK IT collects no data and has no ads or accounts. See our Privacy Policy.
