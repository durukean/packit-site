Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The short version: HALVES collects no data. Nothing leaves your device.

What the app stores

Your best cuts, daily results and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app or use Reset progress in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal information is requested.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No network requests are made during play. The game runs entirely offline.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a Daily Split result, the app hands a short line of text to the iOS share sheet. What happens after that is controlled by the app you share to, not by HALVES.

Children

HALVES is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
